From an operational perspective, application upgrades should continue to follow the standard change management process and be implemented under an approved CAB.

Unlike signature or vulnerability database updates, application upgrades involve changes to the application itself and may require service downtime, pre-change validation, and a system snapshot or backup to provide a rollback option should any issues arise during the upgrade.

Executing application upgrades without formal change approval and stakeholder communication could introduce unplanned service disruptions, particularly if development or security teams have scheduled scanning activities during the maintenance window.

If the intention is to automate vulnerability database/signature updates rather than application upgrades, we can review the vendor's supported approach and assess whether it aligns with our operational and change management requirements.
