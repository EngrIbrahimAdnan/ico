Update:

The upgrade has required additional time due to sequential infrastructure dependencies, including UAT server provisioning, PostgreSQL commissioning, firewall/proxy access, and database authentication configuration. Initial Nexus startup also required troubleshooting of the PostgreSQL connectivity and host-based authentication configuration.

These dependencies have now been resolved, and Nexus 3.95.0-07 is successfully running on UAT with PostgreSQL and accessible from the VDI. Current activities are focused on final UAT configuration, log validation, and repository/blob storage validation before proceeding with the controlled migration.
