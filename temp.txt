With regard to Acunetix application upgrades, my recommendation is that these continue to be managed through the standard change management process rather than enabling automatic installation.

Application upgrades require a planned maintenance window, an approved CAB, and a system snapshot prior to implementation to provide a rollback option should any issues be encountered during the upgrade. In addition, advance communication with the development teams is required to avoid disruption to any planned development or scanning activities.

To ensure the platform remains up to date, we will continue to perform regular checks for new Acunetix releases. When a new version becomes available, we will plan and schedule the upgrade through the normal change management process, ensuring all required stakeholders are informed prior to implementation.
