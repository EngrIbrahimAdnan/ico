Implement Daily Automated Backup for Bitbucket Server




Configure automated daily backups for the Bitbucket server. Bitbucket hosts the organization's developers' source code, making it a critical application that requires regular backups to support business continuity and disaster recovery.

Requirements:

Schedule automated daily backups.
Ensure repositories, configuration, and application data are included.
Store backups securely in accordance with the organization's retention policy.
Periodically validate backup integrity and restoration.
