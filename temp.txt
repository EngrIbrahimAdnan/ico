Provision 1 TB NFS Storage for Bitbucket DR Shared Home



**Purpose:**
Provision 1 TB NFS storage for the Bitbucket DR environment to host the Bitbucket shared home data as part of the DR implementation.

**Requirement:**
The current Bitbucket DR shared home is located under:

`/u01/Atlassian/home/stash/shared`

The current storage consumption is approximately **350 GB**, primarily consisting of:

* `shared/data` – 333 GB
* `shared/shared/search` – 16 GB
* Remaining shared directories – <1 GB

A **1 TB NFS** is requested to accommodate the existing data and provide sufficient capacity for future repository growth.

Kindly provision the NFS storage and provide the required mount/export details and access for the Bitbucket DR server.

The NFS will initially be implemented and validated on the DR environment. Production integration will be addressed separately following successful DR validation.
